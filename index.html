AI Assistant — Sample Questions

This is a prepared demonstration, not a live AI chatbot.

🧠

What is medical AI?

AI systems can help analyze information, recognize patterns and support some healthcare workflows.

🩻

How can AI help doctors?

Some tools can help review medical images or organize information, while clinicians remain responsible for decisions.

🤝

Can AI replace doctors?

No. AI can be a support tool, but human expertise, communication and clinical judgment are essential.

Medical Library

Explore a few ways AI is used in healthcare.

Medical Imaging

AI tools can help specialists examine X-rays, CT scans and other images for patterns that need attention.

Drug Research

Researchers use computational tools, including AI, to study molecules and support parts of the drug-discovery process.

Remote Monitoring

Some digital systems can help track health measurements and flag changes for a care team to review.

Personalized Care

When carefully validated, data tools may help clinicians consider information relevant to an individual patient.
